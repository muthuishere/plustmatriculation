Explain the mode of sex determination in
honey bees?
i) In hymenopteran insects such as honeybees, ants and wasps
a mechanism of sex determination called haplodiploidy
mechanism of sex determination is common.
ii) In this system, the sex of the offspring is determined by the
number of sets of chromosomes it receives.
iii) Fertilized eggs develop into females (Queen or Worker) and
unfertilized eggs develop into males (drones) by
parthenogenesis.
iv) It means that the males have half the number of
chromosomes (haploid) and the females have double the
number (diploid), hence the name haplodiplody for this system
of sex determination.

chains are attached to each other to form a Y shaped structure

(Carboxyl) and amino or N-terminal. Each chain (L and H) has two
