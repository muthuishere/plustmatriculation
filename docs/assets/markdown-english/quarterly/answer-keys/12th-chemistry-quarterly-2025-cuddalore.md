Hume-Rothery Rule :

                To form a substitute alloy the difference between the atomic radii of

solvent and solute is less than 15%. Both the solvent and solute must have the same

crystal structure and valence and their electronegativity difference must be close to zero.

Transition metals satisfying these mentioned conditions form a number of alloys among

themselves, since their atomic sizes are similar and one metal atom can be easily replaced

by another metal atom from its crystal lattice to form an alloy.
