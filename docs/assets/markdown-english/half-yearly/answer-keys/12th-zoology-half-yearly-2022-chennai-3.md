as the leading strand. Replication is discontinuous in
coding strand and known as the lagging strand.
The discontinuously synthesized fragments of the lagging
strand (Okazaki fragments) are joined by the enzyme DNA
ligase.

complementary nucleotides are paired with the existing
nucleotides on the parent strand and covalently bonded

newly synthesized DNA strand.
Finally, all the gaps are sealed by the enzyme DNA ligase.
