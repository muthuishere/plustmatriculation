(i) Explain Gravity separation or hydraulic wash?
Ore with high specific gravity is separated from gaugue with low specific gravity by
simply washing with running water. Finely powdered ore is treated with rapidly
flowing current of water. Lighter gaugue particles are washed away by the running
water. This method is used for concentrating native ore such as gold and oxide ores
such as haematite (Fe2O3), tin stone(SnO2).
