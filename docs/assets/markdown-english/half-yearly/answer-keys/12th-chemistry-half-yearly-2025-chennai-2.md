35(b)Discuss briefly assumptions of  Valence bond theory.(5)

  The ligand→ metal bond in a coordination complex is covalent in nature.

  Each ligand should have at least one filled orbital containing a lone pair of

electrons.

  The central metal ions provides required number of vacant orbitals.

  These vacant orbitals undergo hybridisation.

  The vacant hybridised orbitals of central metal ion, linearly overlap with filled

orbitals of ligands to form coordinate covalent sigma bond.

In the octahedral complexes (n-1) orbitals are involved in hybridisation, then

they are called inner (or) low spin complexes. If the nd orbitals are involved in

hybridisation, such complexes are called outer orbital (or) high spin

complexes.

paramagnetic. If all the electrons are paired, then the complexes will be
diamagnetic.
